Teaching

University of Virginia, 2019–2025 · first-year writing through the Engagements Curriculum

I taught at the University of Virginia from 2019 to 2025, first-year writing through the Engagements Curriculum’s general-education seminars. Food as Art? put its students through blind taste tests and a Mindful Eating Workshop in UVA’s Teaching Kitchen. The midterm in Literary Form and the Philosophical Imagination staged imagined dialogues, West with Plato, Augustine with Nietzsche; we read Woolf’s The Waves beside Merleau-Ponty’s Phenomenology of Perception. Self-Writing Across Genre and Media worked an arc from autofiction through podcasting to autoethnography.
